ZeroMusic

Android 8.0+ // Ad-Free // Status: Active

An ad-free music player for Android. Stream YouTube Music audio without interruptions, import Spotify & YouTube playlists, download songs for offline listening, and follow along with synced lyrics.

Features

Ad-Free Streaming

Search songs, albums and playlists. Clean background playback with lock-screen controls and no ads.

Import Playlists

Sign in to import your whole YouTube Music library, or paste any Spotify or YouTube Music playlist link.

Offline Backup

Keeps 500+ songs on your phone: liked songs, playlists and smart picks for when you have no data.

Made for You

Daily Mixes and recommendations generated from listening history kept private on your device.

Synced Lyrics

Time-synced lyrics that follow along with karaoke highlighting. Tap any line to jump right to it.

Sync with Google Drive

Optional. Back up your library into your own private Google Drive app folder, restored on any device.

Your data

No servers, no tracking, no account requirements. Your library and listening history live on your device. If you sign in with Google, they're stored in your own Google Drive. Read our privacy policy.
