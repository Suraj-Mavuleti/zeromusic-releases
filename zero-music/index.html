Your music, everywhere you go.

ZeroMusic is a free Android music player. Search a huge catalog, bring your playlists over from other services, keep your favourites offline and get recommendations based on what you actually listen to.

Download for Android

Android 8.0 or newer. Free, no account required.

Search & stream

Find songs, albums and playlists and play them with background playback and lock-screen controls.

Import playlists

Paste a Spotify or YouTube Music playlist link to bring it into your library.

Offline backup

Automatically keeps your liked songs, playlists and recommendations on your phone for when you're offline.

Made for you

A daily mix and picks based on your listening, which is stored only on your device.

Synced lyrics

Time-synced lyrics that follow along as you listen.

Sign in with Google

Optional. Keeps your library in your own Google Drive so it follows you to a new phone.

Your data stays yours

ZeroMusic has no servers and no ads or analytics. Your library and listening history live on your phone. If you sign in with Google, they're also stored in a private app folder in your own Google Drive that only ZeroMusic can access. Read the privacy policy.
