ZeroMusic

Android 8.0+ // Free // Status: Active

A music player for Android. Search a huge catalog, bring your playlists over, keep your favourites offline and get recommendations based on what you actually listen to.

Download APK Release notes
Features

Search & stream

Find songs, albums and playlists. Background playback with lock-screen controls.

Import playlists

Sign in to import your whole YouTube Music library, or paste any Spotify or YouTube Music playlist link.

Offline backup

Keeps 500+ songs on your phone: liked songs, playlists and picks for you.

Made for you

A daily mix and recommendations from listening history kept on your device.

Synced lyrics

Time-synced lyrics that follow along. Tap a line to jump to it.

Sync with Google

Optional. Your library in your own Google Drive, restored on a new phone.

Your data

No servers, no ads, no tracking. Your library and listening history live on your phone. If you sign in with Google, they're also kept in a private app folder in your own Google Drive that only ZeroMusic can access. Read the privacy policy.
