What it does

Search and playSongs appear from the first letter. Artists, albums and live playlists, with background playback.
Bring your playlistsImport your YouTube Music library or paste a Spotify or YouTube Music link.
Offline backupKeeps 500 or more songs on your phone and tops itself up, so there's always music without internet.
Made for youYour Mix, Daily Mixes and Discover Weekly, built from listening history that stays on your phone.
Synced lyricsKaraoke-style lines that follow the song, with translation and a lock-screen view.
Listen together and BlendPlay in sync with friends, share playlists, or blend your tastes into one playlist.
Your soundEqualizer, crossfade, speed and pitch, with a separate profile for each pair of headphones.
On the roadCar mode with huge buttons, Android Auto, Chromecast, widgets and a Quick Settings tile.
Smart playlistsMost played, Forgotten favourites and more, kept up to date by themselves.
Sync with GoogleOptional. Your library in your own Google Drive, restored on a new phone.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android. The file Zero-Music.apk downloads straight to your phone.
  2. Open it from your notifications or Downloads. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. That's the only manual step. After that the app updates itself.

Your data

No ads, no tracking, no servers of our own. Your library and listening history live on your phone. If you sign in with Google, they're also kept in a private app folder in your own Google Drive that only Zero-Music can access. Read the privacy policy.
