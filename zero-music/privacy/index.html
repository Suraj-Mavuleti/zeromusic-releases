Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026 (rev. 4) // App: Zero-Music // Status: Active

1. Overview

This policy explains what information the Zero-Music Android app ("Zero-Music", "the app") uses and why. In short: Zero-Music has no servers of its own, shows no ads, has no analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share your data.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

To work, the app keeps the following on your phone, in storage only Zero-Music can access:

  • Your liked songs, imported playlists and downloaded songs.
  • Your listening history (which songs you played, how often, when and for how long), used only for recommendations and your listening stats inside the app. Clear it any time in Settings → Playback → Listening history.
  • Your settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this from your phone.

3. Google Sign-In and Google Drive (Optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google, the app asks Google for two permissions:

PermissionWhat Zero-Music does with it
emailShows which Google account you're signed in with, in Settings. The address is stored only on your phone.
drive.appdataSaves one backup file (liked songs, playlists, listening history and settings) in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive, and reads it back when you sign in on another phone. This permission only covers Zero-Music's own folder: the app cannot see, open or change any other files in your Drive.

This data goes directly between your phone and Google. It never passes through servers run by us, and we never have access to it. The backup is stored under your Google account and governed by Google's privacy policy.

4. YouTube Music Sign-In (Optional)

If you choose Sign in to YouTube Music, you sign in on Google's own page inside the app. Zero-Music never sees your password. After sign-in, the app keeps the resulting YouTube Music session on your phone and uses it only to read your library (your playlists and Liked Music) so it can import them. The session is sent only to music.youtube.com, never to us or anyone else. Settings → YouTube Music library → Sign out deletes it from your phone, and you can end the session any time from your Google account's device list.

5. Limited Use Disclosure

Zero-Music's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup and sync feature described above. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to anyone else, not used to train AI or machine-learning models, and not read by humans.

6. Deleting Your Google Data

  • In the app: Settings → Google account → Sign out stops syncing.
  • To delete the backup file: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps, find Zero-Music and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • To remove Zero-Music's access entirely: visit myaccount.google.com/connections and remove Zero-Music.

7. Services The App Connects To

To play music and provide features, the app connects directly from your phone to these services. They receive your IP address and the request itself (for example, a search term), as any website you visit would, and handle it under their own privacy policies:

  • YouTube / YouTube Music: search, recommendations and audio (Google privacy policy).
  • Spotify: only when you import a Spotify link. The app reads that playlist's public page; it never signs in to Spotify (Spotify privacy policy).
  • LRCLIB (lrclib.net): lyrics. The app sends the song title, artist and length.
  • GitHub: checking for and downloading app updates.
  • ntfy.sh: only for Listen together and shared playlists. Playback updates and playlist changes are encrypted on your phone with a key that exists only in the invite link, then relayed through ntfy.sh. The relay sees only unreadable data and a random room name.
  • Google Translate: only when you tap Translate on lyrics. The lyric text is sent to translate it.
  • Chromecast and cast devices: only when you cast. The phone streams the audio to your cast device over your local network; nothing is sent anywhere else.

8. Permissions

Bluetooth (optional): only if you turn on Auto-play on Bluetooth, so the app can tell when headphones, a speaker or your car connects. It isn't used for anything else. Install apps: lets Zero-Music install its own updates.

9. Children

Zero-Music is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
