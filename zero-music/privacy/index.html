Privacy policy

Effective September 30, 2026

This policy explains what information the ZeroMusic Android app ("ZeroMusic", "the app", "we") uses and why. In short: ZeroMusic has no servers of its own, shows no ads, has no analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share your data.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your phone, in storage only ZeroMusic can access:

  • Your liked songs, imported playlists and downloaded songs.
  • Your listening history (which songs you played, how often and when), used only to make recommendations inside the app. You can clear it at any time in Settings → Playback → Listening history.
  • Your settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this from your phone.

Google sign-in and Google Drive (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google, the app asks Google for two permissions:

PermissionWhat ZeroMusic does with it
emailShows which Google account you're signed in with, in Settings. The address is stored only on your phone.
drive.appdataSaves one backup file (your liked songs, playlists, listening history and settings) in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive, and reads it back when you sign in on another phone. This permission only covers ZeroMusic's own folder: the app cannot see, open or change any other files in your Drive.

This data goes directly between your phone and Google. It never passes through servers run by us, and we never have access to it. The backup is stored under your Google account and governed by Google's privacy policy.

Limited Use disclosure: ZeroMusic's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup and sync feature described above. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to anyone else, not used to train AI or machine-learning models, and not read by humans.

Deleting your Google data

  • In the app: Settings → Google account → Sign out stops syncing.
  • To delete the backup file: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps, find ZeroMusic and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • To remove ZeroMusic's access entirely: visit myaccount.google.com/connections and remove ZeroMusic.

Services the app connects to

To play music and provide features, the app connects directly from your phone to these services. They receive your IP address and the request itself (for example, a search term), as any website you visit would, and handle it under their own privacy policies:

  • YouTube / YouTube Music for search, recommendations and audio (Google privacy policy).
  • Spotify, only when you import a Spotify link or connect a Spotify account (Spotify privacy policy). If you connect an account, the app reads your playlists and liked songs to import them. Your Spotify login tokens are stored only on your phone.
  • LRCLIB (lrclib.net) for lyrics. The app sends the song title, artist and length.
  • GitHub to check for app updates and download them.

Children

ZeroMusic is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
